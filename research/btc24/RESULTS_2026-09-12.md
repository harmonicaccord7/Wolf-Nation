# Bitcoin model milestone: implemented, experimental

The first `K-BTC-24H 0.1.0` model has been trained, chronologically evaluated, independently reproduced and stored. **It did not demonstrate predictive advantage.** This closes the first model-engineering milestone; it does not pass the predictive release gate or complete the other KAPORAL models. PR #21 remains held.

The model forecasts tomorrow's complete 24-hour UTC day. It does **not** forecast an immediate trade from the moment a user opens the page. The input gap is deliberate and identical in historical and prospective use.

The model stayed mostly in cash under the predeclared 0.55 exposure threshold: **only five exposed days** in 985 test days. At 10/25/50 basis points per side, the illustrative model return was +9.74% / +8.10% / +5.42%. Continuous buy-and-hold over the same dates returned +82.21% / +81.66% / +80.75% under its own entry/exit costs. These are simulated returns from daily candle proxies. Five selected historical exposures and zero measured daily-close drawdown do not establish reliability, safety or expected future profit. The probability metrics and their uncertainty provide no demonstrated advantage over the benchmark.

Historical candles are the vendor's current vintage. Original historical publication timestamps and revision vintages are unavailable. No live order execution was simulated, no trade was placed, and no financial conclusion or newsletter was published. Fixed parameters, source behavior, splits, costs, exclusions and acceptance limitations are documented in the [protocol](README.md).

## What remains

Forward evidence requires elapsed time. The baseline's historical failure is retained; later candidates need a stated hypothesis, new version, independent evaluation and separate prospective ledger. Macro, liquidity, volatility, Africa and geopolitics prediction models remain unfinished. The source-backed observation modules already implemented for those desks are not trained predictors.

Human editorial review, authenticated Studio acceptance, actual newsletter issue receipt, Search Console sitemap submission and physical-device acceptance are still separate release requirements. The current application changes must pass CI and remain on the unmerged PR until applicable evidence is complete.
